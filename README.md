# 💸 NS Spendly

**NS Spendly** is a sleek, modern, and privacy-focused personal expense tracker Android application built natively with **Jetpack Compose** and **Material 3**. Spendly empowers users to manage their daily finances effortlessly, categorize expenses, analyze spending trends, and maintain complete ownership of their financial data securely on their device.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Dashboard**: Get an instant overview of your spending summary and recent transactions.
- **Expense Tracking**: Easily log expenses with categories, payment methods, bank accounts, notes, and dates.
- **Advanced Reports**: Visualize spending habits and filter analytics across custom date ranges.
- **Data Backup & Restore**: Export and import your financial records securely via CSV.
- **Theme Customization**: Switch smoothly between Light, Dark, and System themes.

---

## 💡 How to Use

1. **Launch & Onboarding**: Open the app and follow the welcome introduction to get started.
2. **Track Expenses**: Tap to log a new expense with its amount, category, payment mode, and date.
3. **Monitor Dashboard**: View your spending totals and recent transaction history on the home screen.
4. **Analyze Reports**: Navigate to the Reports tab to examine your spending distribution and trends.
5. **Manage Settings**: Visit Settings to customize your theme preferences or export/import your financial data backups.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **UI**: Jetpack Compose & Material 3
- **Architecture**: Modern MVVM Architecture
- **Storage**: Local secure on-device SQLite database
- **Language**: Kotlin & Coroutines
